About Cardiac Sciences

The human heart is an incredible organ responsible for pumping blood throughout the body. Our Cardiac Sciences department utilizes the latest technology to monitor, diagnose, and treat various heart conditions, preventing severe complications.

Did you know?
  • The heart beats about 100,000 times a day.
  • It pumps approximately 2,000 gallons of blood every day.
  • An electrical system controls the rhythm of your heart.

CardioHealth's prediction application uses state-of-the-art Deep Learning models to rapidly assess cardiovascular risk factors. By providing key clinical parameters, you enable our algorithms to perform a rigorous secondary check alongside trusted cardiologists.
